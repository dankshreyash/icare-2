About ICARE
ICARE helps Indian higher education understand how well its institutions perform, and what to do next. We began with a state project, the Karnataka State Universities' Rating Framework (KSURF), which later became the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF).
Since then we have worked with central and state governments, the Prime Minister's Office, leading IITs, large private universities, and the global ranking agencies QS and Times Higher Education, to build frameworks, validate data and turn it into insight that institutions can act on.
Our approach
We do not chase rankings. We help institutions do the right things, so that rankings follow as a byproduct. National ranking data is public, so our value lies in analysis: showing an institution where it stands against peers, how far it is from its goals, and which focus areas will move it most.
Global rankings also depend heavily on academic reputation surveys, which carry about 30% weight in major world rankings. We therefore encourage Indian institutions to support one another's reputation globally while competing at home.
What we do
Ranking and rating frameworks: Designing national and state-level frameworks, from KSURF to NIRF, and state programmes that show how institutions perform within a state.
Accreditation and data validation: Accrediting institutions for the national accreditation authority, and validating and verifying the data they submit.
Civil-service capacity building: Assessing and accrediting civil-service training institutions under Mission Karmayogi, and developing standards with national training institutions, for the Prime Minister's Office.
Institutional analytics: Data analysts embedded in universities, building dashboards and insights so effort goes in the right direction and progress is visible over time.
National and global rankings support: Advising public and private universities on NIRF, QS and Times Higher Education rankings.
Policy and strategy: Recommending and shaping the Institutions of Eminence initiative, and working on the National Education Policy.
Ranking publications: Putting together the rankings published by major Indian news magazines.
Track record
National mandates
NIRF: ICARE recommended and built the National Institutional Ranking Framework, which grew out of the Karnataka State Universities' Rating Framework (KSURF).
Accreditation: Appointed to accredit institutions across the country, and data validation and verification partner of the national accreditation authority (NAAC).
Institutions of Eminence: Recommended and helped create the initiative, and prepared the presentations through which several institutions earned the Eminence title.
National Education Policy: Worked closely on the policy, including with the late Dr Kasturirangan.
Mission Karmayogi: Empanelled by the Prime Minister's Office for the Capacity Building Commission. ICARE assesses and accredits civil-service training institutions across India.
State programmes
The model first built in Karnataka became a national model. ICARE has audited, assessed and rated institutions in Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Haryana, Kerala and Karnataka, among others, and several state governments have asked us to run state-level ranking and rating programmes.
Most recently, the Government of Maharashtra appointed ICARE to work with its seven public state universities, including Mumbai and Pune. ICARE data analysts work inside these universities to build dashboards and insights, so progress can be tracked over time.
Partners
Advisory to leading institutions: Formal official advisors to IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Kanpur and IIT Kharagpur, and to a Bangalore institute. ICARE has traditionally worked with the older IITs on global university rankings.
Private universities: Support to large private universities, including BITS Pilani, Manipal, VIT, SRM, SASTRA, Amrita and OP Jindal, on national and global ranking efforts.
Global ranking agencies: Officially engaged by QS and Times Higher Education in their early years in India.
Media: Works closely with leading Indian news magazines, including Week, Business World and Outlook, whose rankings ICARE puts together.
Governments: Prime Minister's Office, state governments and the national accreditation authority.
Tools and initiatives
AI-based simulators: A what-if tool built on public NIRF data. Enter a change, such as 20 more research papers, and see where it could move an institution.
Benchmarking tools: Shows the distance between an institution and a peer such as IIT Madras, so leaders can choose their focus areas for the year.
TRUTH Index: Tracks research retractions to uphold transparency and honesty.
Stanford top-2% list analysis: ICARE decodes and analyses the widely cited list.
Global Rankings Outreach Group: Being set up at IIT Madras to bring Indian institutions together on global reputation, in talks with the Ministry of Education.
